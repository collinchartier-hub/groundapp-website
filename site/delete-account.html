Delete your GroundApp account

GroundApp · Developer: Collin Chartier · Last Updated: October 25, 2026

GroundApp is a breathwork app for iPhone, Apple Watch and Android, made by Collin Chartier. You can delete your account and the data tied to it two ways: inside the app, or by email if you cannot open the app. Both work for iPhone and Android accounts.

Option 1: Delete it in the app

  • iPhone: Profile > Account > Delete account
  • Android: Settings > Delete Account. You will be asked to type DELETE to confirm.

Deletion starts as soon as you confirm, and you are signed out. Data synced to our servers is permanently removed within 30 days.

Option 2: Delete it without the app

Email privacy@groundapp.live from the email address on your account, with the subject Delete my GroundApp account. We will confirm by reply and delete your account within 30 days.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it with the store you bought it from: Apple subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions. We never see your card details, so we cannot cancel it for you.

What gets deleted

From our servers (hosted by Supabase), within 30 days:

  • Your account and profile
  • Your session history
  • Your saved check-ins
  • Your saved sequences
  • Advanced apnea training tables, references, results and deletion records (iPhone only)
  • Your notification records
  • Your daily Aura token counts
  • The Apple transaction ID linked to your account, which we keep so one Premium subscription unlocks Aura for one account

If you delete in the app, the app also clears the local data tied to that account on your phone.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Product analytics already sent to PostHog: Not removed automatically. We keep it only as long as we need it to understand how the App is used, and we delete it if you ask. Email privacy@groundapp.live.
  • Copies of your email held by our email providers: Deleted on request, at the same address. If you joined the email list and later unsubscribed, we keep a record that you unsubscribed so we do not email you again. To delete that too, email us.
  • Aura server logs (iPhone): They hold a request ID, a one-way hash of your account ID, the AI model used, timing, size counts and whether the request worked, and no check-in content. We keep them only as long as needed to run and secure the service.
  • Aura requests at Anthropic: Anthropic deletes them within 30 days of the request. If its safety systems flag a request as breaking its Usage Policy, it can keep that request for up to 2 years, or longer if the law requires it.
  • Data only on your phone: Account deletion does not erase everything on your device. Guest data and other device-only data stay until you erase it. On iPhone, delete the App. On Android, uninstall it or go to Android Settings > Apps > GroundApp > Storage > Clear storage.
  • Health Connect records (Android): Exercise sessions the App wrote to Health Connect stay there until you delete them in the Health Connect app. We keep no copy of them.

The full details are in our Privacy Policy (see Your Rights and Controls, and Data Retention). Questions? Email privacy@groundapp.live.
