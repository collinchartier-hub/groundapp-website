Accessibility

groundapp.live · Statement revised for the private preview: November 14, 2026

We want everyone to be able to use groundapp.live, whether you use a keyboard, a screen reader, captions, zoom or reduced motion. This page describes our design aim, current review status, known limits and how to reach us.

The standard

WCAG level AA is a design aim, not a claim of conformance or legal certification. This statement covers the website. The native iPhone and Apple Watch apps, store pages and external destinations need their own reviews.

Review scope and status

This version changes the home page, blog index and five articles, three Breath Atlas practices, browser game, waiting room, support and policy pages, and missing-page screen. A current route-by-route keyboard, screen-reader, zoom and reduced-motion audit has not yet been completed for this preview. No page count or automated pass score is claimed.

Earlier checks do not establish that this changed version passes. A reproducible review needs the route, viewport, browser, assistive technology, date, method and observed result. Screenshots alone do not establish accessibility.

Controls designed into this version

  • Skip links, visible focus and labels for form fields.
  • Home-page motion pause and static poster states for reduced motion.
  • User-initiated audio, native audio controls and existing written Atlas transcripts.
  • Keyboard and touch game inputs, pause and sound controls. Game movement is not a breathing instruction.

What still falls short

  • Browser game: menus and buttons are text, but the 3D action is not conveyed completely to a screen reader. Keyboard timing is still part of play. This is not equivalent access to all gameplay.
  • Bodie and app demos: canvas and screen imagery do not expose all their visual detail. Descriptions are provided, but the demos are not an assistive-technology test of the native app.
  • The intro film: some captions are part of the picture and cannot be resized or hidden. The existing transcript is retained; its medical-sounding statements are not endorsed as clinical guidance.
  • Atlas recordings: existing transcripts have editorial limitations noted beside them. Cadence and full transcript accuracy still need recording review. Access forms are not exercised against live services in this preview.
  • Zoom and mobile: full 200%/400% zoom, VoiceOver and short-phone checks remain to be recorded.

Tell us about a problem

If something does not work for you, email collin@groundapp.live. Include the page, device, browser, assistive technology if used, and what happened. Ask for an alternative format if that would help. No response deadline is promised.

If you are in Norway and you're not happy with our answer, you can contact Uutilsynet, the Norwegian authority for universal design of ICT, at uutilsynet.no.
