Racing taught me that fitness is only half of it. The other half is your nervous system: whether you can stay calm when it counts, and whether you can come back down after.

Your breath is the one part of that system you can steer on purpose. I built GroundApp to make that simple. You check in, say how you feel, and it gives you the breathwork that fits.
