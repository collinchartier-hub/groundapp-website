Garmin app privacy

GroundApp for Garmin · Effective November 13, 2026

Scope

This policy covers GroundApp for Garmin, the standalone Connect IQ watch app created by Collin Chartier. It is separate from GroundApp on iPhone and Android. Those apps and this website are covered by our main privacy policy.

Data on your watch

The watch app stores your practice preferences, a paused practice checkpoint and your latest 20 session summaries on the watch. A summary includes the practice, start time, active duration, completed cycles and Garmin activity-recording status. If live heart rate is enabled and readings are available, it also includes the average, minimum, maximum and count of those readings.

Optional heart rate

Live heart rate is off by default. You can enable it in the watch app's Preferences, subject to Garmin permissions and sensor availability. GroundApp samples heart rate only while a practice is running, stops sampling while paused or ended, and does not display stale readings as live. These summaries stay on the watch and are not sent to GroundApp.

Garmin activity recording

Garmin Activity is on by default and can be switched off in Preferences. When enabled, GroundApp uses Garmin's native recorder to save a Meditation/Breathing FIT activity. Garmin may include device-managed sensor data according to Garmin and device settings, independently of GroundApp's live heart-rate display toggle. Garmin handles synchronization to your Garmin Connect account under Garmin's own privacy policy and your Garmin settings.

If you exit while paused, the recorder saves that activity segment. Resuming after reopening starts a new segment. GroundApp does not upload directly to Strava. Any sharing from Garmin Connect to Strava, Apple Health or another connected service depends on the integrations you enable with Garmin.

No GroundApp server collection

This watch app has no GroundApp account, phone companion, network permission, advertising or analytics. It does not send your preferences, session history or heart-rate readings to a GroundApp server. It does not access your location, contacts, microphone or photos. It does not sync with GroundApp on your phone or use AI services.

Retention and deletion

The app keeps only the latest 20 local session summaries. Preferences and paused progress remain until replaced or removed. To remove the app's local data, uninstall GroundApp from your watch using Garmin's app controls. Uninstalling does not delete activities already saved by Garmin. Delete those separately through Garmin's activity controls. GroundApp has no server copy to delete.

Contact and changes

For privacy requests or questions, email privacy@groundapp.live. For app support, email collin@groundapp.live. We will update this page if the Garmin app's data practices change. Future versions that add server sharing will describe that change before it is enabled.
