Privacy policy

GroundApp · Effective Date: March 8, 2026 · Last Updated: November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

GroundApp ("we," "our," or "the App") is a breathwork app for iPhone, Apple Watch and Android, created by Collin Chartier. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the App and our website, groundapp.live (the "Website"), including health data from Apple Health, Health Connect and connected devices.

By using the App or the Website, you consent to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App and the Website.

2. Information we collect in the app

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Account information: An account is required to use GroundApp on iPhone. You can sign in with Apple (iPhone only), with Google, or with your email address and a password; for email, we send a 6-digit code to confirm the address, and another if you reset your password. We store your email address, the sign-in method you used and, if Apple or Google gives it to us, your name. Apple gives us your name only the first time you sign in, and if you choose Apple's Hide My Email we receive Apple's relay address instead. Email sign-up does not ask for a name. Your password goes to our sign-in service, Supabase, over an encrypted connection and is not kept in the App. GroundApp stores these details in your account profile (Section 4.2). We use them to run your account, to send account emails such as sign-in codes, and to send GroundApp's own emails (Section 3); every marketing email has an unsubscribe link. We never sell them and never use them for tracking. On Android you can also skip sign-in; the App then tries to create an anonymous account with no name or email.
  • Goals and experience level: The goals and experience level you choose, if you choose them (on iPhone, Profile > Practice preferences). On iPhone and on Android these stay on your device. Versions of the iPhone App before 1.2.0 uploaded them to your profile; if you used one, that earlier copy may still be stored on our servers until you delete your account.
  • Check-ins: The feeling you choose on the feelings wheel, how intense it is, and your energy level. If you use Bodie's AI plans (called Aura in iPhone versions before 1.4.0; see Section 4.4), these are also sent to build your plan (Section 4.4). On Android, asking for a check-in plan always sends them (Section 4.5).
  • Session reflections: Ratings, how you feel after a session, and any notes you write. On iPhone, ratings and notes stay on your device; the feeling you pick after a session syncs with your session history (Section 4.2).
  • Saved sequences: Breathing sequences you build and save.
  • Advanced apnea training (iPhone only): Table names and settings, reference holds you enter or record, whether a hold was done with Full or Empty lungs, recorded hold and recovery times, round outcomes, posture and preparation context, readiness-confirmation version and time, and any effort rating or notes you choose to save. These are self-reported training records, not sensor-verified breath holds or medical assessments. Condition-screening answers are used for the current start decision and are not saved.
  • BOLT test results: If you take the BOLT test, your score and the date. On iPhone and on Android these stay on your device.

2.2 Health and biometric data

With your explicit permission, the App accesses the following data. You choose what to allow, and you can change it at any time. On iPhone, Apple Health stays off until you turn it on, during setup or in Profile > Health and devices > Apple Health.

Apple Health, read:

  • Heart rate and resting heart rate
  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Respiratory rate
  • Sleep analysis
  • Blood oxygen saturation (SpO2) and VO2 max
  • Body temperature and sleeping wrist temperature
  • Active energy burned and step count
  • Mindful minutes

Apple Health, write: Mindful Minutes and workout records for your breathing sessions. On Apple Watch, the App reads your heart rate during a session and saves the session as a workout.

What the App does with Apple Health on your phone: It shows your health dashboard and nervous system insights and keeps daily snapshots on your device (Section 4.1). If you also leave Profile > Health and devices > Check-in > Include Apple Health context on, your HRV and sleep readings help choose and soften the plan after a check-in, on your phone. If Apple Health is on, the App may also look at your readings on your phone, when iOS lets it refresh in the background, to send you a suggestion notification. This dashboard and suggestion processing happens on your phone. For automatic AI planning, only the word labels described in Section 4.4 are sent, and only if you enable them. Separately, you can choose to share a session card containing heart readings (Section 2.5), export local session data (Section 9), or include local data in your device backup (Section 4.1).

Health Connect (Android), read: With your explicit permission, the App reads these record types from Health Connect, Android's on-device health data store:

  • Heart rate: the last 24 hours. The App shows your lowest reading as resting heart rate on your Progress screen.
  • Sleep sessions: the last 24 hours, added up into hours slept for your Progress screen.
  • Steps: the App asks for permission to read steps, but does not currently read them.

Health Connect (Android), write: Exercise sessions. If you turn on Auto-Save Sessions in Settings > Health Connect, the App saves each finished breathing session to Health Connect as a yoga exercise session, with its start time, end time and a title that begins "Ground:" followed by the technique name. Health Connect data is read and used on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers and is never sent to Aura, Anthropic or analytics (Section 6).

Wearables (Whoop, Garmin, Oura, COROS and others): GroundApp does not connect to any wearable maker's service. If your wearable syncs to Apple Health, the App can read that data through Apple Health, like any other Apple Health data listed above (Section 7).

Bluetooth heart-rate straps: If you pair a strap, the App reads your live heart rate during sessions. On iPhone, the App asks for Bluetooth permission when you set up a monitor in Profile > Health and devices > Bluetooth heart rate monitor, remembers the monitor's name and identifier on your device so it can reconnect, and saves fresh heart-rate samples about every five seconds with the session. If the monitor supplies usable beat-to-beat intervals, the App also calculates and saves heart rate variability samples at about that interval. These readings are stored on your device only: they are not uploaded to our servers, sent to analytics or sent to Bodie. On Android, the App asks for Bluetooth permission to find and connect to a strap that supports the standard Bluetooth Heart Rate Service, shows the readings live, and does not upload them to our servers.

Apple Watch: The Watch app does not contact any server itself. During a session it reads your heart rate and passes it to the iPhone App and, with your Apple Health permission, saves the session as a workout. When a session finishes, it sends the technique, length and cycles to the iPhone App, which treats it like any other session (Section 4.2).

2.3 Automatically collected information

  • Usage data (product analytics): Screens viewed, app opens and closes, and in-app events such as techniques started and finished, session length, check-ins, programs, shares, and paywall and purchase events. On iPhone this also covers how long you stay on a screen, check-in type, music and voice choices, intro video views and trial offers. These events do not include health readings, your name, your email, how you feel, or anything you type. They are linked to a random account ID, not your email. On iPhone and Android, the App asks once, after setup ("Help improve GroundApp?"), and sends no product analytics until you answer. Until you say yes, no product analytics are sent, in every country, and that includes any device ID. "Don't share" keeps it off. You can change your answer anytime in Profile > Privacy and data > Share usage data on iPhone, or Settings > Privacy > Share Usage Data on Android. Turning it off stops sending right away.
  • Device information: Device model, operating system version and app version, sent along with product analytics events.
  • Approximate (coarse) location: Your city or country, worked out by our analytics provider from your internet address, only if Share Usage Data is on. We never collect your precise location.
  • Notification token (iPhone only): While you are signed in, the App registers with Apple's push notification service and uploads a push notification token for your device to our servers, along with your account ID, the platform and the app version. This happens whether or not you have allowed notifications; if you have not, iOS does not show them. When you sign out, the App tries to remove the token from our servers. If that request fails, the server copy can remain until your account is deleted. Account deletion removes it. The server also keeps a record of each comeback reminder it sent you: which one, its text and when (Section 4.2). The Android App does not use push notifications and has no notification token: its reminders are scheduled on your device (Section 3).
  • Connection data: Like any online service, our servers and the providers in Section 4.3 receive your IP address and basic request data when the App connects.

2.4 Information we do NOT collect

  • Precise geolocation
  • Contacts or address book data
  • Browsing history
  • Payment card or billing details. Purchases are handled entirely by the app store: Apple on iPhone, and Google Play (Google Play Billing) on Android. We never see your card details. On iPhone, to provide Premium and to send required subscription reminders, our server receives your subscription status from Apple and keeps it with its Apple transaction ID (Sections 4.4 and 4.6).
  • Advertising identifiers. Neither the iPhone App nor the Android App collects them. The iPhone App does not include the Meta SDK, does not show Apple's tracking prompt, and sends no ad measurement events or advertising identifiers to Meta or any ad network. The App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. (The Website is separate: it uses the Meta Pixel to measure our ads. See Section 8.)

2.5 Sharing a session (iPhone)

If you tap Share on a finished session, the App builds a picture of your session card on your phone. It shows your first name (if the App knows it), a short line from a set we wrote, the technique or plan name, the time, the date and, only if the session has heart data and you leave "Show heart rate" on, your starting and ending heart rate and heart rate variability. The picture goes only where you send it: the iOS share sheet, Photos ("Save image") or the Instagram or Facebook Story editor. GroundApp does not receive the picture. If you allow product analytics, we record the share option or destination app, not the picture or its contents.

"Save image" asks for add-only access to Photos: the App can add the picture and cannot read your photos. For Instagram and Facebook, the App puts the picture on your iPhone's clipboard and opens that app. The App holds a public Meta app ID that Instagram and Facebook require for this. It contains no Meta SDK and sends no advertising measurement events to Meta. Choosing Instagram or Facebook hands the card to that app.

4. How we store and protect your data

4.1 On-device storage

  • Your sessions, check-ins, session ratings and notes, heart-rate and heart rate variability samples, BOLT scores, favorites and saved sequences, preferences, and Advanced apnea records are stored on your device, in a store kept for the account you are signed in to. Signed-in history also syncs as described below (Section 4.2); session ratings and notes, heart-rate samples, BOLT scores and unfinished apnea checkpoints stay local.
  • If you used GroundApp on this iPhone before an account was required (before version 1.3.0), the first time you create an account or sign in, the App moves the history on the phone into that account: sessions, check-ins, BOLT scores, favorites, saved sequences, Advanced apnea tables, references and unfinished training, practice preferences and reminders. It then syncs like any other account data. Apple Health, Bodie and monitor choices are not carried over. An item that conflicts with one already in the account stays on the phone.
  • Daily health snapshots built from Apple Health data are kept on your device for up to 90 days.
  • If Share usage data is on, up to 1,000 recent usage events (up to 90 days old) are also kept on your device and are part of Export all data.
  • GroundApp does not use iCloud or CloudKit to store your data. If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, that backup may include data the App stores on your device.
  • Widgets use a small summary stored on your device and shared only with the App's own widgets: your streak, total minutes, last session date, today's intention and a suggested technique.
  • On Android, the App keeps your sessions, check-ins, settings and BOLT scores in a database and preferences on your device. Android may back up that app data to your Google account through Android's own backup (Auto Backup), depending on your device settings; that backup may include data the App stores on your device.

4.2 Cloud storage (account sync)

When you are signed in, the App automatically syncs the following to our servers (hosted by Supabase, using PostgreSQL):

  • Your profile: email, sign-in method, name (if Apple or Google gave it to us) and last active date
  • Your session history: technique, duration, cycles, the mood you picked, the time of the session, and the check-in it followed, if any
  • Saved check-ins: selected feelings and their broader labels, intensity and energy, the nervous system state the App works out from your answers, check-in entry type, date, time and time zone, and saved plan information such as its title, affirmation and reframe. This private history sync is separate from optional product analytics and from asking Bodie (Aura on Android) to generate a plan.
  • Advanced apnea training: saved table names and settings, manually entered or recorded reference holds, whether Full or Empty lungs was used, and finalized session results including planned and recorded hold/recovery times, round outcomes, context, date and time zone, session plan revisions, record revision numbers, and optional effort ratings and notes
  • Breathing sequences you save
  • Your push notification token, with your account ID, platform and app version, while you are signed in (Section 2.3)
  • A record of each comeback reminder we sent you: which one, its text and when

Raw health readings from Apple Health (including data from wearables that sync to it) or a heart-rate strap are not uploaded to our servers. Saved check-in answers and plan text sync as private history, including any context reflected in that text. If you choose Bodie's AI plans, the request and separately permitted coarse Apple Health labels pass through our server to Anthropic; the request processor does not keep a separate copy of the prompt or response. Saved history is distinct from this transient processing. The server also keeps your daily plan count and your subscription status from Apple, including its Apple transaction ID (Sections 4.4 and 4.6).

Apnea records sync only to your own signed-in account. Account sync cannot be turned off separately, because an account is required to use the App on iPhone, so declining analytics or turning Bodie's AI plans off does not turn it off. Apnea times, references, notes, effort and screening answers are not sent to product analytics or Bodie. Condition-screening answers are not saved; records retain the confirmation version/time and posture/preparation context. Deleting a saved apnea table or reference clears its stored contents; a minimal deletion record with account ID, record ID, revision and timestamps remains to prevent an old offline copy from returning. A past session keeps its original snapshot. To request deletion of an individual finalized result from your account, email privacy@groundapp.live. Account deletion removes these account records.

On Android, signed-in account sync is smaller. It covers: your profile (name, email and chosen voice guide); your session history (technique ID and name, duration, the feelings you picked after the session, and when it finished); and saved check-ins (mood before and after, energy and stress levels, emotion tags and any note). If you are offline, these wait on your device and sync when you are back online. Not uploaded from Android: onboarding goals and experience level, session ratings and reflection notes, saved sequences, BOLT scores, live heart-rate readings, and Health Connect data. The Android App has no Advanced apnea training.

Our servers are protected as follows:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2+
  • All data at rest is encrypted using AES-256
  • Row-level security ensures you can only access your own data

4.4 Bodie's AI plans (iPhone)

Names in iPhone versions before 1.4.0. In iPhone versions before 1.4.0, this feature is called Aura. The switch in Profile > Privacy and data is "Aura check-ins". The consent screen is "Meet Aura" and its agree button is "Use Aura". The Apple Health switch is "Let Aura use rough Apple Health signals" on the consent screen and "Let Aura use Apple Health signals" in Profile > Privacy and data. The Help row is "What Aura is". Wherever this policy gives a 1.4.0 name for an iPhone control, use this list for the older name.

Bodie's AI plans are the App's optional AI planning service. They recommend breathwork for how you feel right now. They are part of GroundApp Premium; no request is sent to Anthropic until you choose "Use Bodie's AI plans".

This section describes Bodie's AI plans on iPhone. They work differently on Android, where the feature is called Aura; see Section 4.5.

Asking first. Before the first eligible online request to Bodie's AI (you have Premium, are signed in and are online), and again if the required consent changes, the App shows "How Bodie plans your session" (called "Meet Aura" in iPhone versions before 1.4.0) before anything is sent to Anthropic. "Use Bodie's AI plans" agrees. "Not now" builds the plan on your phone instead and sends nothing to Anthropic. This does not turn off the separate signed-in sync of saved history (Section 4.2). Your other paid features keep working either way. You can read the same page later in Profile > Help > How Bodie plans your session.

Plans a day. Bodie's AI builds up to 3 plans a day. After that, or if Bodie's AI cannot be reached, the plan is built on your phone from the same answers. From version 1.4.0, after the check-in the App shows the plan (its title, the minutes, an affirmation and each technique with its duration and why it fits) before you tap Begin session, and plans made by Bodie's AI are labelled "Made by Bodie's AI". The plan screen collects no extra data.

What is sent when Bodie's AI plans are on:

  • Your check-in: the feeling you pick on the feelings wheel and the broader feelings it sits under, how intense it is (1 to 10), your energy (low, medium or high), and the nervous system state the App works out from those answers
  • The time of day as a broad window (such as "morning"). Your local date goes to our server only, to count your daily plans
  • Only if you also turn on the Apple Health switch ("Let Bodie use rough Apple Health signals" on the consent screen, "Let Bodie use Apple Health signals" in Profile > Privacy and data; off by default), with Apple Health and Include Apple Health context on: up to six word labels the App works out on your phone using recent Apple Health data, personal baselines where available, and fixed sleep thresholds. The labels are: HRV lower than usual, HRV higher than usual, resting heart rate higher than usual, short sleep, poor sleep quality, and several poor nights in a row. No numbers are sent.
  • To our server only, never to Anthropic: your sign-in token, so the server knows the request is yours, and Apple's signed record of your Premium subscription, so the server can confirm Premium

Never sent to Anthropic: your name, your email, your account ID, anything you type, apnea training records, or any health numbers such as heart rate, HRV or hours of sleep. Bodie's labels come only from Apple Health data written by Apple's own sources (iPhone, Apple Watch and the Health app); readings that other apps or wearables write to Apple Health are not used for them.

Where it goes. The App sends the request over an encrypted connection to our server (a Supabase Edge Function). Our server checks that you are signed in, have Premium and have plans left today. It then asks Anthropic's Claude to build your plan and sends the plan back to your phone.

What the Bodie request processor keeps. It does not keep a separate copy of your check-in answers, Apple Health labels or generated plan. Saved check-in and plan history sync separately as described in Section 4.2. The request service keeps:

  • Your daily plan count: your account ID, the date and how many Bodie plans you used that day (up to 3)
  • Your subscription status from Apple, including the Apple transaction ID of your Premium subscription, linked to your account, so one subscription unlocks Bodie's AI plans for one account (Section 4.6). We never see your payment details.
  • Server logs: a request ID, a one-way hash of your account ID, the AI model used, timing, size counts and whether the request worked. Logs never include your answers, your labels or your plan.

What Anthropic does with it. Anthropic processes Bodie requests for us under its commercial terms. Anthropic does not train its models on them. Anthropic deletes them within 30 days, unless its automated safety systems flag a request as breaking its Usage Policy (Anthropic can then keep it for up to 2 years) or the law requires it to keep it longer. Anthropic does not receive your name, email, account ID or purchase record.

Changing your mind. Turn Bodie's AI plans off at any time in Profile > Privacy and data > Bodie's AI plans, and stop the Apple Health labels in Profile > Privacy and data > Let Bodie use Apple Health signals. To keep Apple Health out of check-in plans completely, including plans built on your phone, turn off Profile > Health and devices > Check-in > Include Apple Health context. Turning Bodie's AI plans off stops new Bodie requests, not your separate account-history backup. Requests already sent are deleted by Anthropic as described above. If we ever change what Bodie sends, the App asks you again first.

Wellness, not medical advice. Bodie's plans are AI-generated wellness guidance. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

4.5 Aura on Android

On Android, Aura is built differently and gives you less protection for what you type, so read this part. There is no separate "Meet Aura" screen and no Aura on/off switch in Settings yet. Aura has two parts:

  • Aura chat: a conversation where you tell Aura how you feel and it recommends a sequence. Every message you type is sent to Anthropic, along with Aura's earlier replies (the whole conversation is sent again with each new message so Aura remembers it). The App also sends its own instructions: a list of the App's breathing techniques, a broad time-of-day window such as "morning" worked out from your phone's clock, and an opening line the App adds itself ("Hello, I'd like to do a breathwork session."). Anything you type is sent, including anything personal or health related that you choose to write. Please don't type what you do not want to share.
  • Check-in plan: when you finish a check-in and ask for your plan, the App sends the feeling you picked and the broader feelings it sits under, how intense it is (1 to 10), your energy level, the nervous system state the App works out from your feeling, and the same broad time-of-day window. If the request fails, the App builds a plan on your phone instead.

Never sent to Anthropic from Android: your name, your email, your account ID, your Health Connect data (heart rate, sleep, steps), live heart-rate readings from a strap, your saved history, or anything you type anywhere other than the Aura chat.

Where it goes. The Android App sends these requests over an encrypted connection from your phone directly to Anthropic's Claude API, not through our servers. We do not receive or keep a copy of the prompts or replies, and Android has no Aura token count. Anthropic sees your IP address, as any web service would. Anthropic processes these requests for us under its commercial terms, does not train its models on them, and deletes them within 30 days, unless its automated safety systems flag a request as breaking its Usage Policy (Anthropic can then keep it for up to 2 years) or the law requires it to keep it longer.

Changing your mind. To stop anything being sent, do not open Aura chat and do not ask for a check-in plan. Requests already sent are deleted by Anthropic as described above. Aura's plans are AI-generated wellness guidance, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
