Privacy Policy

GroundApp · Effective Date: March 8, 2026 · Last Updated: October 26, 2026

1. Introduction

GroundApp ("we," "our," or "the App") is a breathwork app for iPhone, Apple Watch and Android, created by Collin Chartier. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the App and our website, groundapp.live (the "Website"), including health data from Apple Health, Health Connect and connected devices.

By using the App or the Website, you consent to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App and the Website.

2. Information We Collect in the App

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account information: Your name and email address when you create an account. You can sign in with Apple (iPhone only), with Google, or with an email and password. Apple gives us your name only the first time you sign in. GroundApp stores your name and email in your account profile (Section 4.2). We use them to run your account and to send GroundApp's own marketing emails, and every email has an unsubscribe link. We never sell them and never use them for tracking. On Android you can also skip sign-in; the App then tries to create an anonymous account with no name or email.
  • Onboarding answers: Your breathwork goals and experience level. On Android these stay on your device.
  • Check-ins: The feeling you choose on the feelings wheel, how intense it is, and your energy level. If you use Aura, these are also sent to build your plan (Section 4.4). On Android, asking for a check-in plan always sends them (Section 4.5).
  • Session reflections: Ratings, how you feel after a session, and any notes you write.
  • Saved sequences: Breathing sequences you build and save.
  • Advanced apnea training (iPhone only): Table names and settings, reference holds you enter, recorded hold and recovery times, round outcomes, posture and preparation context, readiness-confirmation version and time, and any effort rating or notes you choose to save. These are self-reported training records, not sensor-verified breath holds or medical assessments. Condition-screening answers are used for the current start decision and are not saved.

2.2 Health and Biometric Data

With your explicit permission, the App accesses the following data. You choose what to allow, and you can change it at any time.

Apple Health (HealthKit), read:

  • Heart rate and resting heart rate
  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Respiratory rate
  • Sleep analysis
  • Blood oxygen saturation (SpO2) and VO2 max
  • Body temperature and sleeping wrist temperature
  • Active energy burned and step count
  • Mindful minutes

Apple Health (HealthKit), write: Mindful Minutes and workout records for your breathing sessions. On Apple Watch, the App reads your heart rate during a session and saves the session as a workout.

Health Connect (Android), read: With your explicit permission, the App reads these record types from Health Connect, Android's on-device health data store:

  • Heart rate: the last 24 hours. The App shows your lowest reading as resting heart rate on your Progress screen.
  • Sleep sessions: the last 24 hours, added up into hours slept for your Progress screen.
  • Steps: the App asks for permission to read steps, but does not currently read them.

Health Connect (Android), write: Exercise sessions. If you turn on Auto-Save Sessions in Settings > Health Connect, the App saves each finished breathing session to Health Connect as a yoga exercise session, with its start time, end time and a title that begins "Ground:" followed by the technique name. Health Connect data is read and used on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers and is never sent to Aura, Anthropic or analytics (Section 6).

Wearables (Whoop, Garmin, Oura, COROS and others): GroundApp does not connect to any wearable maker's service. If your wearable syncs to Apple Health, the App can read that data through Apple Health, like any other Apple Health data listed above (Section 7).

Bluetooth heart-rate straps: If you pair a strap, the App reads your live heart rate during sessions. On iPhone, these readings are stored on your device only. On Android, the App asks for Bluetooth permission to find and connect to a strap that supports the standard Bluetooth Heart Rate Service, shows the readings live, and does not upload them to our servers.

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage data (product analytics): Screens viewed, app opens and closes, and in-app events such as techniques started, session length, ratings, check-in type, music and voice choices, and paywall and purchase events. These events do not include health readings, your name, your email, or anything you type. They are linked to a random account ID, not your email. On iPhone and Android, the App asks once, after setup ("Help improve GroundApp?"), and shares nothing until you answer. Until you say yes, nothing is sent, in every country, and that includes any device ID. "Don't share" keeps it off. You can change your answer anytime in Settings > Privacy > Share Usage Data. Turning it off stops sending right away.
  • Device information: Device model, operating system version and app version, sent along with product analytics events.
  • Approximate (coarse) location: Your city or country, worked out by our analytics provider from your internet address, only if Share Usage Data is on. We never collect your precise location.
  • Notification token (iPhone only): If you allow notifications, a push notification token for your device, along with your account ID and app version. The Android App does not use push notifications and has no notification token: its reminders are scheduled on your device (Section 3).

2.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Precise geolocation
  • Contacts or address book data
  • Browsing history
  • Payment card or billing details. Purchases are handled entirely by the app store: Apple on iPhone, and Google Play (Google Play Billing) on Android. We never see your card details. On iPhone, to confirm Premium for Aura, our server checks Apple's signed record of your subscription and keeps its Apple transaction ID (Section 4.4).
  • Advertising identifiers. The App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. (The Website uses the Meta Pixel to measure our ads. See Section 8.)

4. How We Store and Protect Your Data

4.1 On-Device Storage

  • Health readings, check-ins, session reflections and notes, heart-rate samples, and Advanced apnea records are stored on your device. Guest history stays local unless you explicitly choose to move it into an account. Signed-in history also syncs as described below; unfinished apnea checkpoints stay local.
  • Daily health snapshots built from Apple Health data are kept on your device for up to 90 days.
  • GroundApp does not use iCloud or CloudKit to store your data. If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, that backup may include data the App stores on your device.
  • On Android, the App keeps your sessions, check-ins, settings and BOLT scores in a database and preferences on your device. Android may back up that app data to your Google account through Android's own backup (Auto Backup), depending on your device settings; that backup may include data the App stores on your device.

4.2 Cloud Storage (Account Sync)

When you are signed in, the App automatically syncs the following to our servers (hosted by Supabase, using PostgreSQL):

  • Your profile: name, email, onboarding goals, experience level, and last active date
  • Your session history: technique, duration, cycles, the mood you picked, the time of the session, and the check-in it followed, if any
  • Saved check-ins: selected feelings and their broader labels, intensity and energy, the nervous system state the App works out from your answers, check-in entry type, date, time and time zone, and saved plan information such as its title, affirmation and reframe. This private history sync is separate from optional product analytics and from asking Aura to generate a plan.
  • Advanced apnea training: saved table names and settings, manually entered reference holds, and finalized session results including planned and recorded hold/recovery times, round outcomes, context, date and time zone, session plan revisions, record revision numbers, and optional effort ratings and notes
  • Breathing sequences you save
  • Your notification token, account ID and app version, if you allow notifications

Raw health readings from Apple Health (including data from wearables that sync to it) or a heart-rate strap are not uploaded to our servers. Saved check-in answers and plan text sync as private history, including any context reflected in that text. If you choose Aura, its request and separately permitted coarse Apple Health labels pass through our server to Anthropic; the request processor does not keep a separate copy of the prompt or response. Saved history is distinct from this transient processing. The server also keeps your daily Aura token count and your subscription's Apple transaction ID (Section 4.4).

Apnea records sync only to your own signed-in account. Declining analytics or turning Aura off does not turn off this account backup; stay a guest if you do not want account sync. Apnea times, references, notes, effort and screening answers are not sent to product analytics or Aura. Condition-screening answers are not saved; records retain the confirmation version/time and posture/preparation context. Deleting a saved apnea table or reference clears its stored contents; a minimal deletion record with account ID, record ID, revision and timestamps remains to prevent an old offline copy from returning. A past session keeps its original snapshot. To request deletion of an individual finalized result from your account, email privacy@groundapp.live. Account deletion removes these account records.

On Android, signed-in account sync is smaller. It covers: your profile (name, email and chosen voice guide); your session history (technique ID and name, duration, the feelings you picked after the session, and when it finished); and saved check-ins (mood before and after, energy and stress levels, emotion tags and any note). If you are offline, these wait on your device and sync when you are back online. Not uploaded from Android: onboarding goals and experience level, session ratings and reflection notes, saved sequences, BOLT scores, live heart-rate readings, and Health Connect data. The Android App has no Advanced apnea training.

Our servers are protected as follows:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2+
  • All data at rest is encrypted using AES-256
  • Row-level security ensures you can only access your own data

4.4 Aura (AI Check-In)

Aura is the App's optional AI planning service. It recommends breathwork for how you feel right now. It is part of Ground Premium; no request is sent to Anthropic until you choose Use Aura.

This section describes Aura on iPhone. Aura works differently on Android; see Section 4.5.

Asking first. When you first ask for an Aura plan, the App shows "Meet Aura" before your answers are sent to Anthropic. If you tap "Not now", it builds a plan on your phone instead. This does not turn off the separate signed-in sync of saved history (Section 4.2). Your other paid features keep working either way.

What is sent when Aura is on:

  • Your check-in: the feeling you pick on the feelings wheel and the broader feelings it sits under, how intense it is (1 to 10), your energy (low, medium or high), and the nervous system state the App works out from those answers
  • The time of day as a broad window (such as "morning") and your local date, which is used to count your daily Aura tokens
  • If you tap "Less time" or "Alternatives": that request and which techniques to leave out
  • Only if you also turn on "Let Aura use rough Apple Health signals" (off by default): up to six word labels the App works out on your phone by comparing recent Apple Health data with your own usual levels. The labels are: HRV lower than usual, HRV higher than usual, resting heart rate higher than usual, short sleep, poor sleep quality, and several poor nights in a row. No numbers are sent.
  • To our server only, never to Anthropic: your sign-in token, so the server knows the request is yours, and Apple's signed record of your Premium subscription, so the server can confirm Premium

Never sent to Anthropic: your name, your email, anything you type, apnea training records, or any health numbers such as heart rate, HRV or hours of sleep. Aura's labels come from Apple Health data only.

Where it goes. The App sends the request over an encrypted connection to our server (a Supabase Edge Function). Our server checks that you are signed in, have Premium and have Aura tokens left. It then asks Anthropic's Claude to build your plan and sends the plan back to your phone.

What the Aura request processor keeps. It does not keep a separate copy of your check-in answers, Apple Health labels or generated plan. Saved check-in and plan history sync separately as described in Section 4.2. The request service keeps:

  • Your daily Aura token count: your account ID, the date and how many Aura plans you used that day (up to 3)
  • The Apple transaction ID of your Premium subscription, linked to your account, so one subscription unlocks Aura for one account. We never see your payment details.
  • Server logs: a request ID, a one-way hash of your account ID, the AI model used, timing, size counts and whether the request worked. Logs never include your answers, your labels or your plan.

What Anthropic does with it. Anthropic processes Aura requests for us under its commercial terms. Anthropic does not train its models on them. Anthropic deletes them within 30 days, unless its automated safety systems flag a request as breaking its Usage Policy (Anthropic can then keep it for up to 2 years) or the law requires it to keep it longer. Anthropic does not receive your name, email, account ID or purchase record.

Changing your mind. Turn Aura off at any time in Settings > Privacy > Aura check-ins, and stop the Apple Health labels in Settings > Privacy > Let Aura use Apple Health signals. Turning Aura off stops new Aura requests, not your separate account-history backup. Requests already sent are deleted by Anthropic as described above. If we ever change what Aura sends, the App asks you again first.

Wellness, not medical advice. Aura's plans are AI-generated wellness guidance. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

4.5 Aura on Android

On Android, Aura is built differently and gives you less protection for what you type, so read this part. There is no separate "Meet Aura" screen and no Aura on/off switch in Settings yet. Aura has two parts:

  • Aura chat: a conversation where you tell Aura how you feel and it recommends a sequence. Every message you type is sent to Anthropic, along with Aura's earlier replies (the whole conversation is sent again with each new message so Aura remembers it). The App also sends its own instructions: a list of the App's breathing techniques, a broad time-of-day window such as "morning" worked out from your phone's clock, and an opening line the App adds itself ("Hello, I'd like to do a breathwork session."). Anything you type is sent, including anything personal or health related that you choose to write. Please don't type what you do not want to share.
  • Check-in plan: when you finish a check-in and ask for your plan, the App sends the feeling you picked and the broader feelings it sits under, how intense it is (1 to 10), your energy level, the nervous system state the App works out from your feeling, and the same broad time-of-day window. If the request fails, the App builds a plan on your phone instead.

Never sent to Anthropic from Android: your name, your email, your account ID, your Health Connect data (heart rate, sleep, steps), live heart-rate readings from a strap, your saved history, or anything you type anywhere other than the Aura chat.

Where it goes. The Android App sends these requests over an encrypted connection from your phone directly to Anthropic's Claude API, not through our servers. We do not receive or keep a copy of the prompts or replies, and Android has no Aura token count. Anthropic sees your IP address, as any web service would. Anthropic processes these requests for us under its commercial terms, does not train its models on them, and deletes them within 30 days, unless its automated safety systems flag a request as breaking its Usage Policy (Anthropic can then keep it for up to 2 years) or the law requires it to keep it longer.

Changing your mind. To stop anything being sent, do not open Aura chat and do not ask for a check-in plan. Requests already sent are deleted by Anthropic as described above. Aura's plans are AI-generated wellness guidance, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

In accordance with Apple's HealthKit guidelines:

  • We do not use HealthKit data for advertising or similar services
  • We do not sell HealthKit data to third parties, including advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers
  • We do not use HealthKit data for any purpose other than providing health and wellness services directly to you
  • We share HealthKit data with a third party only through Aura, and only if you turn on its separate Apple Health switch: a few word labels worked out on your phone, never the numbers, sent through our server to Anthropic (Section 4.4). Never for advertising
  • Raw HealthKit readings and the coarse labels themselves are not stored on our servers. Aura's labels pass through the request processor to Anthropic without a separate stored copy. Plan text you save may reflect those labels and syncs as private history (Section 4.2). GroundApp does not use iCloud or CloudKit for this storage; your own device backup may include locally stored data (Section 4.1).
  • You can revoke HealthKit access at any time through iOS Settings

6. Health Connect Compliance

GroundApp's use of information received from Health Connect will adhere to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

For the Android App, that means:

  • We do not use Health Connect data for advertising or similar services, including retargeting and interest-based ads
  • We do not sell Health Connect data to third parties, including ad platforms, data brokers, or information resellers
  • We do not use Health Connect data to determine credit-worthiness or insurance eligibility
  • We do not transfer Health Connect data to anyone except as needed to provide the features you see in the App. Today that means no one: heart rate and sleep are read and used on your device, and are not sent to our servers, Anthropic, analytics or any other third party. If you type your own health details into Aura chat, that is text you typed, and it is sent like any other message (Section 4.5).
  • We use Health Connect data only to provide features visible in the App: showing your lowest heart rate and hours slept from the last 24 hours on your Progress screen, and saving your breathing sessions back to Health Connect if you turn on Auto-Save Sessions
  • Nobody at GroundApp reads your Health Connect data, because it never leaves your device
  • The App reads two record types: heart rate and sleep sessions. It asks for permission to read steps but does not currently read them. It writes one: exercise sessions (Section 2.2)
  • Access happens only with your permission. You can revoke it at any time in the Health Connect app under App permissions > GroundApp (Section 9). When you do, the App stops reading and writing. Exercise sessions the App already wrote stay in Health Connect until you delete them there.

7. Wearables Through Apple Health

GroundApp does not connect to Oura, WHOOP, Garmin, COROS or any other wearable maker's service. Data from Whoop, Garmin, Oura, COROS and other wearables reaches the App only through Apple Health, and only the data types you allow. The App does not log in to a wearable maker's account and does not contact a wearable maker's cloud.

  • Wearable data in Apple Health is treated like any other Apple Health data in this policy: it is read on your device, covered by Section 5, and never sold or used for advertising
  • Raw readings stay on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. Only if you turn on Aura's separate Apple Health switch, up to six word labels (never numbers) go through our server to Anthropic (Section 4.4)
  • Turning Apple Health off in the App stops new reads. Readings the App already saved stay on your device until you delete your account or the App. Daily health snapshots are kept for up to 90 days (Section 4.1)
  • Earlier versions of the App let you log in to Oura or WHOOP. Version 1.2.0 removed both. The first time you open 1.2.0, the App deletes any Oura or WHOOP login tokens still on your device
  • What a wearable writes to Apple Health is set in that wearable's own app. You control what GroundApp can read in iOS Settings (Section 9)

8. Website and Email List

This section covers groundapp.live, including the Breath Atlas pages and the blog.

  • Email list signups: When you join the email list, we collect your email address, your first name if you give it, and which signup form you used (for example, the homepage, the blog or a Breath Atlas page). This is stored in our database, hosted by Supabase.

  • Spam protection: Our signup form uses automated checks to block spam and abuse, which may include rate limits based on your IP address.
  • Analytics: We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites. It measures things like which pages were visited, the referring site, browser and device type, and country.
  • App Store button taps: When you tap an App Store button, we count which button it was and on which page, in our database (hosted by Supabase), so we know what works. We do not store who tapped it.
  • Ad measurement (Meta Pixel): If you visit from outside the EU, the UK and Switzerland, the Website also uses the Meta Pixel from Meta (Facebook and Instagram) to measure our ads and show them to people likely to be interested. It records the pages you visit and your taps on App Store buttons, and it uses cookies. When you tap an App Store button, our server also tells Meta about the tap, with your IP address, browser type and Meta’s cookies. Nothing from the App, including health data, is ever sent to Meta. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, the Website does not load the pixel or tell Meta about taps. You can also block cookies, use an ad blocker, or manage the ads Meta shows you at facebook.com/adpreferences.
  • Hosting: The Website is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard request data, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver and secure the site.
  • Browser storage: The Website saves a few small preferences in your browser's local storage, such as whether you closed the email popup or unlocked a Breath Atlas practice. This stays in your browser and is not sent to us.

To have your email removed from our list entirely, email privacy@groundapp.live.

  • On-device data: Kept until you erase it or delete the App; account-scoped data is also cleared after confirmed account deletion. Daily health snapshots are kept for up to 90 days. On Android, uninstalling the App or clearing its storage erases it.

11. Data Security and Breach Notification

11.1 Security Measures

We employ commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your data, consistent with GDPR Article 32 standards. These include:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.2+) and of data at rest on our servers (AES-256)
  • iOS Keychain storage for authentication tokens
  • Row-level security for cloud-stored data
  • Access controls limited to authorized personnel

11.2 Breach Notification
