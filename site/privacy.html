Privacy Policy

GroundApp · Effective Date: March 8, 2026 · Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

GroundApp ("we," "our," or "the App") is a breathwork app for iPhone and Apple Watch, created by Collin Chartier. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the App and our website, groundapp.live (the "Website"), including health data from Apple Health and connected devices.

By using the App or the Website, you consent to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App and the Website.

2. Information We Collect in the App

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account information: Your name and email address when you create an account. You can sign in with Apple, with Google, or with an email and password.
  • Onboarding answers: Your breathwork goals and experience level.
  • Check-ins: The feeling you choose on the feelings wheel, how intense it is, and your energy level. If you use Aura, these are also sent to build your plan (Section 4.4).
  • Session reflections: Ratings, how you feel after a session, and any notes you write.
  • Saved sequences: Breathing sequences you build and save.

2.2 Health and Biometric Data

With your explicit permission, the App accesses the following data. You choose what to allow, and you can change it at any time.

Apple Health (HealthKit), read:

  • Heart rate and resting heart rate
  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Respiratory rate
  • Sleep analysis
  • Blood oxygen saturation (SpO2) and VO2 max
  • Body temperature and sleeping wrist temperature
  • Active energy burned and step count
  • Mindful minutes

Apple Health (HealthKit), write: Mindful Minutes and workout records for your breathing sessions. On Apple Watch, the App reads your heart rate during a session and saves the session as a workout.

Oura Ring (Oura API, connected with your Oura login):

  • Daily readiness
  • Daily sleep and sleep sessions
  • Daily stress and resilience
  • Heart rate

Garmin: GroundApp does not connect to Garmin directly. If your Garmin device syncs to Apple Health, the App can read that data through Apple Health, like any other Apple Health data listed above.

Bluetooth heart-rate straps: If you pair a strap, the App reads your live heart rate during sessions. These readings are stored on your device only.

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage data (product analytics): Screens viewed, app opens and closes, and in-app events such as techniques started, session length, ratings, check-in type, music and voice choices, and paywall and purchase events. These events do not include health readings, your name, your email, or anything you type. They are linked to a random account ID, not your email. The App asks once, after setup ("Help improve GroundApp?"), and shares nothing until you answer. "Don't share" keeps it off. You can change your answer anytime in Settings > Privacy > Share Usage Data.
  • Device information: Device model, operating system version and app version, sent along with product analytics events.
  • Approximate location: Your city or country, worked out by our analytics provider from your internet address, only if Share Usage Data is on. We never collect your precise location.
  • Notification token: If you allow notifications, a push notification token for your device, along with your account ID and app version.

Oura Usage Data Disclosure: When you connect your Oura Ring to GroundApp, Oura may collect certain Usage Data related to your use of the Oura API and platform. Oura may use this Usage Data for its own business purposes as described in Oura's Privacy Policy.

2.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Precise geolocation
  • Contacts or address book data
  • Browsing history
  • Payment card or billing details. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. To confirm Premium for Aura, our server checks Apple's signed record of your subscription and keeps its Apple transaction ID (Section 4.4).
  • Advertising identifiers. The App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. (The Website uses the Meta Pixel to measure our ads. See Section 7.)

4. How We Store and Protect Your Data

4.1 On-Device Storage

  • Health readings, check-ins, session reflections and notes, and heart-rate samples are stored on your device.
  • Daily health snapshots built from Apple Health and Oura data are kept on your device for up to 90 days.
  • Oura sign-in tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • GroundApp does not use iCloud or CloudKit to store your data. If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, that backup may include data the App stores on your device.

4.2 Cloud Storage (Account Sync)

When you are signed in, the App automatically syncs the following to our servers (hosted by Supabase, using PostgreSQL):

  • Your profile: name, email, onboarding goals, experience level, and last active date
  • Your session history: technique, duration, cycles, the mood you picked, and the time of the session
  • Breathing sequences you save
  • Your notification token, account ID and app version, if you allow notifications

Health readings from Apple Health, Oura or a heart-rate strap are not uploaded to our servers. If you use Aura, your check-in and any Apple Health labels pass through our server on the way to Anthropic without being stored, and our server keeps your daily Aura token count and your subscription's Apple transaction ID (Section 4.4). Our servers are protected as follows:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2+
  • All data at rest is encrypted using AES-256
  • Row-level security ensures you can only access your own data

4.4 Aura (AI Check-In)

Aura is the App's AI check-in. It recommends breathwork for how you feel right now. Aura is part of Ground Premium, and nothing is sent until you say yes.

Asking first. The first time you check in, the App shows a "Meet Aura" screen before anything leaves your phone. It explains what is sent, where it goes and how to turn it off. If you tap "Not now", nothing is sent and the App builds your plan on your phone from your answers. Everything you paid for keeps working either way.

What is sent when Aura is on:

  • Your check-in: the feeling you pick on the feelings wheel and the broader feelings it sits under, how intense it is (1 to 10), your energy (low, medium or high), and the nervous system state the App works out from those answers
  • The time of day as a broad window (such as "morning") and your local date, which is used to count your daily Aura tokens
  • If you tap "Less time" or "Alternatives": that request and which techniques to leave out
  • Only if you also turn on "Let Aura use rough Apple Health signals" (off by default): up to six word labels the App works out on your phone by comparing recent Apple Health data with your own usual levels. The labels are: HRV lower than usual, HRV higher than usual, resting heart rate higher than usual, short sleep, poor sleep quality, and several poor nights in a row. No numbers are sent.
  • To our server only, never to Anthropic: your sign-in token, so the server knows the request is yours, and Apple's signed record of your Premium subscription, so the server can confirm Premium

Never sent: your name, your email, anything you type, Oura or WHOOP data, or any health numbers such as heart rate, HRV or hours of sleep. Aura's labels come from Apple Health data only. Readings that came from Oura or WHOOP are left out.

Where it goes. The App sends the request over an encrypted connection to our server (a Supabase Edge Function). Our server checks that you are signed in, have Premium and have Aura tokens left. It then asks Anthropic's Claude to build your plan and sends the plan back to your phone.

What our server keeps. Our server does not store your check-in answers, your Apple Health labels or your Aura plans. It keeps only:

  • Your daily Aura token count: your account ID, the date and how many Aura plans you used that day (up to 3)
  • The Apple transaction ID of your Premium subscription, linked to your account, so one subscription unlocks Aura for one account. We never see your payment details.
  • Server logs: a request ID, a one-way hash of your account ID, the AI model used, timing, size counts and whether the request worked. Logs never include your answers, your labels or your plan.

What Anthropic does with it. Anthropic processes Aura requests for us under its commercial terms. Anthropic does not train its models on them. Anthropic deletes them within 30 days, unless its automated safety systems flag a request as breaking its Usage Policy (Anthropic can then keep it for up to 2 years) or the law requires it to keep it longer. Anthropic does not receive your name, email, account ID or purchase record.

Changing your mind. Turn Aura off at any time in Settings > Privacy > Aura check-ins, and stop the Apple Health labels in Settings > Privacy > Let Aura use Apple Health signals. Turning Aura off stops anything new from being sent. Requests already sent are deleted by Anthropic as described above. If we ever change what Aura sends, the App asks you again first.

Wellness, not medical advice. Aura's plans are AI-generated wellness guidance. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

5. Apple HealthKit Compliance

In accordance with Apple's HealthKit guidelines:

  • We do not use HealthKit data for advertising or similar services
  • We do not sell HealthKit data to third parties, including advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers
  • We do not use HealthKit data for any purpose other than providing health and wellness services directly to you
  • We share HealthKit data with a third party only through Aura, and only if you turn on its separate Apple Health switch: a few word labels worked out on your phone, never the numbers, sent through our server to Anthropic (Section 4.4). Never for advertising
  • We do not store HealthKit data in iCloud, CloudKit or on our servers. Aura's Apple Health labels pass through our server on the way to Anthropic without being stored there
  • You can revoke HealthKit access at any time through iOS Settings

6. Oura Data Usage Compliance

In accordance with the Oura API Agreement:

  • Oura data is accessed only with your explicit authorization via OAuth 2.0
  • We request only the scopes the App needs: daily (readiness, stress and resilience), heart rate, and sleep
  • Oura data is used only to provide features in the App, such as your health dashboard. It is never sent to Aura or Anthropic
  • Oura data is stored on your device as part of the daily health snapshots described in Section 4.1, kept for up to 90 days
  • You can disconnect Oura at any time in the App's Settings, which deletes the App's Oura login tokens from your device. You can also revoke access at cloud.ouraring.com
  • We do not sell, license, lease, or market your Oura data, and we do not share it with any third party, including advertisers, data brokers and AI services such as Anthropic.
  • In the event of a data security incident involving Oura data, we will notify Oura within 24 hours of discovery

7. Website and Email List

This section covers groundapp.live, including the Breath Atlas pages and the blog.

  • Email list signups: When you join the email list, we collect your email address, your first name if you give it, and which signup form you used (for example, the homepage, the blog or a Breath Atlas page). This is stored in our database, hosted by Supabase.
  • Emails: We send the welcome email and newsletter emails with Resend, an email delivery service. Every email has an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribe any time and we will stop sending you newsletter emails.
  • Spam protection: Our signup form uses automated checks to block spam and abuse, which may include rate limits based on your IP address.
  • Analytics: We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites. It measures things like which pages were visited, the referring site, browser and device type, and country.
  • App Store button taps: When you tap an App Store button, we count which button it was and on which page, in our database (hosted by Supabase), so we know what works. We do not store who tapped it.
  • Ad measurement (Meta Pixel): If you visit from outside the EU, the UK and Switzerland, the Website also uses the Meta Pixel from Meta (Facebook and Instagram) to measure our ads and show them to people likely to be interested. It records the pages you visit and your taps on App Store buttons, and it uses cookies. When you tap an App Store button, our server also tells Meta about the tap, with your IP address, browser type and Meta’s cookies. Nothing from the App, including health data, is ever sent to Meta. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, the Website does not load the pixel or tell Meta about taps. You can also block cookies, use an ad blocker, or manage the ads Meta shows you at facebook.com/adpreferences.
  • Hosting: The Website is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard request data, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver and secure the site.
  • Browser storage: The Website saves a few small preferences in your browser's local storage, such as whether you closed the email popup or unlocked a Breath Atlas practice. This stays in your browser and is not sent to us.

To have your email removed from our list entirely, email privacy@groundapp.live.

9. Data Retention

  • On-device data: Retained until you delete the App. Daily health snapshots are kept for up to 90 days.
  • Cloud-synced data: Retained while your account is active; deleted within 30 days of account deletion
  • Aura requests: Not stored on our servers. Anthropic deletes them within 30 days, with the exceptions in Section 4.4
  • Aura token counts and Premium transaction ID: Kept while your account is active; deleted when you delete your account
  • Aura server logs: Contain no check-in content; kept only as long as needed to run and secure the service

  • Email list: Kept while you are subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we keep a record that you unsubscribed so we do not email you again. To delete it entirely, email us.

10. Data Security and Breach Notification

10.1 Security Measures

We employ commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your data, consistent with GDPR Article 32 standards. These include:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.2+) and of data at rest on our servers (AES-256)
  • iOS Keychain storage for authentication tokens
  • Row-level security for cloud-stored data
  • Access controls limited to authorized personnel

10.2 Breach Notification

In the event of a security breach affecting your personal or health data:

  • We will notify affected users within 60 days of discovery, or sooner as required by applicable law
  • We will notify Oura within 24 hours of discovery (per the Oura API Agreement)
  • We will notify the FTC and applicable state regulators as required by the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule and state law
  • Notifications will describe the nature of the breach, the types of data involved, the steps we are taking, and how you can protect yourself
