I built GroundApp because I wanted a clear place to start between coached sessions. A coached session has someone in the room. On an ordinary day, I wanted something you could open without having to figure out what to do first.

Questions, answered

What is GroundApp?

GroundApp is a breathwork app for iPhone and Apple Watch, created by performance breathwork coach Collin Chartier. It offers check-ins with Bodie, guided breathing and session history. Free to download, with a free tier and optional Premium.

What does GroundApp cost?

GroundApp is free to download. Published US Premium pricing is $69.99 a year or $6.99 a month. Your store shows the current local price, included features and any eligible trial before purchase. An Apple subscription renews unless cancelled.

What do I get for free?

There is a free tier to start a practice. See the app for current free inclusions and Premium features before subscribing. We do not promise a fixed catalog or free-technique count.

Who is Bodie?

Bodie is your breathing companion. He sits in the middle of Home and follows the selected technique. Keep any practice comfortable and breathe normally whenever you want.

How does the check-in work?

Tap Check in on Home. Name how you feel, then rate its intensity and your energy. GroundApp uses your answers to suggest a short session. This is guidance, not a diagnosis or measurement of your nervous system.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Create one when you start, with Apple, Google or email. You can delete your account any time in Settings.

Is that really Collin's voice?

The guided sessions use an AI model of my voice, trained on my own recordings. If you'd rather not hear a voice at all, haptic-only mode lets you breathe with vibrations.

Where can I learn about breathwork?

Read The science of breathwork and Nervous system regulation, or try the guided audio in The Breath Atlas: Coherence.

What are the Advanced tables, and are they safe?

O2 and CO2 tables are breath-hold training. You can start from a ready-made table or build one from your own reference hold, you begin each hold, and you can stop any time. Dry land only. Never in or near water. Intense techniques show a short safety note before you start. If you have a heart, lung or other medical condition, check with your doctor before you begin a new breathing practice.

Which devices and wearables does it work with?

GroundApp runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The Apple Watch app (watchOS 11 or later) breathes with Bodie on your wrist. Apple Health reads your heart rate, HRV, sleep, steps and active energy, and saves your sessions as mindful minutes. For live heart rate and HRV in a session, connect a standard Bluetooth heart rate monitor, or a Whoop or Garmin with heart rate broadcast on. Whoop, Garmin, Oura, COROS and other wearables also reach GroundApp through Apple Health. Readings inform suggestions. They are not a medical assessment.

How do I cancel Premium?

Premium is an Apple subscription, so you manage it on your iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then GroundApp. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Where is my data, and can I delete it?

You can export your data as CSV or JSON and delete your account any time in Settings. For exactly what is collected, where it is stored and who processes it, read our privacy policy.

Is this therapy?

No. This is coaching and education, not medical or mental health treatment. If you're dealing with panic attacks, trauma, or thoughts of hurting yourself, start with a licensed professional. In the US you can call or text 988 any time.

Is Bodie's Breath Run free?

Yes. It is a free browser game at groundapp.live/play. Use finger or keyboard controls while breathing normally. It is not a breath-hold test.
