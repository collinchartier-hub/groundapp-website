    Breathwork that starts with how you feel

    GroundApp is built on years of Collin's breathwork classes and coaching. Tell it how you feel and it points you to a session that fits, so you spend your time breathing, not scrolling a library.

    1. 1

      Check in

      Name what you're feeling on a 102-feeling wheel, then rate your intensity and energy. It's optional and it's quick.

    2. 2

      Get matched

      Your check-in matches you to a breathing session built for that state. Anxious and wired gets something different than flat and foggy.

    3. 3

      Breathe with Collin

      Guided sessions in Collin's voice, with ambient sound and haptic cues. Afterward, rate it, note how you feel, and watch your streak and mood trend build.

    Questions, answered

    What does GroundApp cost?

    GroundApp is free to download. GroundApp Premium is $29.99 a year and opens all 35 techniques, 15 guided sessions and the daily check-in.

    What do I get for free?

    Four techniques are free forever: Box Breathing, Deep Slow Breathing, 2‑to‑1 Breathing and Triangle Breathing. You also get the breathing timer, streaks, basic stats and the BOLT CO2 tolerance test.

    Is that really Collin's voice?

    The guided sessions use an AI model of Collin's voice, trained on his recordings. Collin Chartier, a breathwork and mental performance coach, designed every session. Prefer a different voice? You can switch the narration to Jane or Oliver.

    Which devices and wearables does it work with?

    GroundApp runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The Apple Watch app (watchOS 11 or later) puts Box, Physiological Sigh, Resonance and Instant Calm on your wrist, with haptic breath cues and live heart rate. Apple Health, Oura and Bluetooth heart-rate straps connect too. Garmin data comes in through Apple Health.

    Does it work offline?

    Yes. Bundled sessions work without a connection, and Instant Calm (one tap, no voice) works offline too.

    Do I need an account?

    Yes. You sign in with Apple, Google or email when you set up the app. You can delete your account any time in Settings.

    How do I cancel Premium?

    Premium is an Apple subscription, so you manage it on your iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then GroundApp. You keep Premium until the end of your current billing period.

    Where is my data, and can I delete it?

    You can export your data as CSV or JSON and delete your account any time in Settings. For exactly what is collected, where it is stored and who processes it, read our privacy policy.
