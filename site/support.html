GroundApp · Developer: Collin Chartier · Last Updated: October 28, 2026

Contact

Email collin@groundapp.live. We reply within 2 business days.

Quick answers

Manage or cancel Premium

On your iPhone, open Settings > your name > Subscriptions.

Restore purchases

In the app, go to Paywall > Restore purchases.

Redeem an offer code

In the app, go to Paywall > Redeem offer code. You can also go to Settings > App Store > Redeem Gift Card or Code.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Profile > Account > Delete account. You can also use the delete account page.

Wearables

Whoop, Garmin, Oura, COROS and others connect through Apple Health. Turn on Apple Health syncing in the wearable's app, then go to Profile > Health and devices > Apple Health in GroundApp.
