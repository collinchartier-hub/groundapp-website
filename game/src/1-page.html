    How to play

    One button. Real breathing.

    Race Kip

    Tap to jump. Press and keep holding to hold your breath: Bodie's belly glows, he floats, walks through Worry Clouds and dives under logs. Let go to breathe out and refill. Hold too long and he goes PFFFT. Breathe in a Worry Cloud and he POPS. Hold 4 seconds for a Box Breath and get Flow.

    Course techniques

    Sigh Swamp: double tap to do a physiological sigh and blow storms away. Power Peaks: on a power pad, tap tap tap for power breaths, then hold through the thin air. Starfall Temple: ride an updraft, then hold to glide through the rhythm rings.

    The Big Hold

    Thumb down, breath held, Bodie dives. Let go when you need air. In Dash, tap with a second finger (or ↑) to swim up past jellyfish and coral while your first finger keeps holding. On a keyboard, hold Space or the mouse button.

    Breath Surf

    Five techniques: resonance breathing, box breathing, 4-7-8, the physiological sigh and power breaths. Press as you breathe in, let go as you breathe out, and stay on the ribbon.

    Space ↑ W, click or tap. Duel: Player 1 is W/Space or the left half of the screen, Player 2 is ↑/L or the right half. P pauses.

    Five courses, five techniques

    Root Hollow and Still Lake

    Box breathing and breath-hold dives. Hold through Worry Clouds, dive under logs, and hold 4 seconds for a Box Breath and a burst of Flow.

    Sigh Swamp and Power Peaks

    The physiological sigh (two breaths in, one long breath out) blows storms away. Power breaths charge your lungs past 100% for the long hold through thin air.

    Starfall Temple and The Big Hold

    Updrafts and rhythm rings test your glide control. Then push your real max hold in the deep, and beat your own flags on the sea floor.
