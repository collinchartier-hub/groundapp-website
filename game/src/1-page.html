    Race Kip

    Tap to jump. Keep the button pressed for a screen shield through clouds. Release to recharge. The meter is game charge, not your lungs. Breathe normally throughout.

    Course controls

    Double tap for a gust. Tap on power pads to charge the shield. Press after an updraft to glide through rings. There is no underwater or maximal-hold mode.

    Ribbon ride

    Press to move Bodie's cushion up; release to let it settle. There is no score or breath matching. Pause or leave whenever you want.

    Space ↑ W, click or tap. Duel: Player 1 is W/Space or the left half of the screen, Player 2 is ↑/L or the right half. P pauses.

    A game, not a breath test

    Screen controls only

    Tap, press and release with your finger, mouse or keyboard. Never hold or change your breath to play. Game points describe button play, not health or fitness.

    Pause whenever you want

    Use Pause or P to stop a run. Choose Sound and Voice for audio. Reduced motion stills the menu; a run moves only after you start it.

    The maximal-hold mode is removed

    The old Big Hold and its records are not carried into this version. There is no dive challenge, maximal-hold reward or physiological target.
