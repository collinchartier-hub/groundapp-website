Your nervous system already knows how to settle. GroundApp gives it the right breath.

Meet Bodie, your breathing companion. Bodie sits in the middle of Home and breathes the rhythm the wheel lands on. Scroll the wheel to pick a technique, tap Start, or press and hold on Bodie, then breathe with him. No account needed to begin. Collin's 3-minute intro film shows why he made GroundApp and how to use it.

CHECK IN WITH BODIE
Tap Check in on Home. Name how you feel, rate how strong it is and your energy. GroundApp builds a short session from your answers. Anxious and wired gets something different from flat and foggy.

LIBRARY
Techniques: more than 40, including Box Breathing, 4-7-8, Physiological Sigh, Resonance Breathing and Cyclic Sighing. Each has a practice guide: purpose, steps, length and safety.
Advanced: O2 and CO2 tables for breath-hold training. Start from a ready-made table or build one from your own reference hold. You begin each hold and can stop any time. Dry land only. Never in or near water.
Guided: sessions in Collin's voice, with ambient sound and haptic cues.
Sleep sounds: rain, ocean waves, brown noise, a crackling fire and more.

PROGRESS
Track your streaks and session history. Take the BOLT test, a comfortable breath hold timed in seconds, and watch your scores over time. Training history keeps your Advanced tables and holds.

LIVE HEART RATE
Tap the heart button in the player to connect any standard Bluetooth heart rate monitor, or a Whoop or Garmin with heart rate broadcast on. Watch live heart rate and HRV (RMSSD) as you breathe, then get a recap and charts. HRV needs beat-to-beat data.

APPLE HEALTH AND WEARABLES
Turn on Apple Health and GroundApp reads heart rate, HRV, sleep, steps and active energy to suggest the right breathwork for today, and saves your sessions to Health as mindful minutes. Whoop, Garmin, Oura, COROS and other wearables also reach GroundApp through Apple Health: sync them there, then switch it on here. Readings inform suggestions. They are not a medical assessment.

PRACTICE YOUR WAY
Set the session timer from 3 to 20 minutes, a custom length or no limit. Layer in music, nature sounds, voice guidance and haptics, each with its own volume. Haptic-only mode is for silent practice.

APPLE WATCH
Practice on your wrist with the Apple Watch app.

FREE AND PREMIUM
Free: 4 core techniques, the session timer, streaks, basic stats, check-ins with Bodie and live heart rate. GroundApp Premium adds all techniques, the Advanced O2 and CO2 tables and guided sessions. Use GroundApp as a guest, or sign in with Apple or Google.

GROUNDAPP PREMIUM SUBSCRIPTION
GroundApp Premium is an auto-renewing yearly subscription at $39.99 per year in the US. Eligible new subscribers get a 3-day free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel any time in Settings > your name > Subscriptions.
