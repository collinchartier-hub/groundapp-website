# App Store description: corrected draft (2026-10-02)

Paste into App Store Connect with the next submission (1.1.1). Every claim matches shipped v1.1.0 code (fact sheet from the 2026-10-02 website audit).

What changed vs the live listing, and why:
- Removed "AI-powered", "AI-guided intake", "the AI reads your signals": the AI check-in has shown canned text since the model was retired 2026-06-15. Recommendations are rule-based.
- Removed "voice cloning", "guided programs", "community and leaderboards": none ship to users.
- Removed WHOOP (not configured) and direct Garmin (it comes through Apple Health).
- "33 techniques" -> 35 (what the library shows, not counting the Box Breathing variants).
- Removed "Ground does not track you. Your data stays on your device": the app syncs to Supabase and sends analytics to PostHog. This line could get the listing flagged against the privacy label.
- Data export, biometric insights and analytics are not paywalled in code, so they're no longer listed as Premium perks.
- Also fix the screenshots: screenshot 3 ("03_progress") is a duplicate of the Practice screenshot, both Practice shots show an "Offline Mode" banner, and the voice pickers show "Paul", a voice the app no longer has (VoiceGuide.swift lists Collin, Jane, Oliver).

Your nervous system already knows how to settle. GroundApp gives it the right breath to do it.

GroundApp is a breathwork app built by breathwork and mental performance coach Collin Chartier, for people who want to feel calm, focused and present in their bodies. Stress, sleep, energy, focus: pick what you need and breathe.

35 BREATHING TECHNIQUES

From beginner friendly to advanced. Box Breathing, 4-7-8, Physiological Sigh, Cyclic Sighing, Coherent and Resonance Breathing, Alternate Nostril, Wim Hof (simplified), Breath of Fire, Humming Bee, Vagal Toning and more, across calming, energizing, focusing and balancing. Every technique comes with a clear how-to so you know what to do and why it works.

GUIDED SESSIONS IN COLLIN'S VOICE

15 guided sessions narrated in Collin's voice (an AI voice model trained on his recordings), plus Jane and Oliver. Prefer silence? Go haptic only.

CHECK IN, THEN BREATHE

Tap how you feel on a 102-feeling wheel, set your intensity and energy, and GroundApp matches you to a breathing session for that state. After each session, rate it and note how you feel.

QUICK FIXES AND INSTANT CALM

One tap for Sleep, Anxiety, Focus, Energy, Headache or Stress. Instant Calm runs with no voice and works offline.

SOUND THAT HELPS

6 ambient soundscapes, binaural beats (delta, theta, alpha, beta), 32 music tracks, a sleep sounds player and 4 transition bells. Voice and music volume adjust separately.

APPLE WATCH, HEALTH AND WEARABLES

Breathe on your wrist with haptic cues and live heart rate. GroundApp reads HRV, resting heart rate, sleep and respiratory rate from Apple Health and logs Mindful Minutes back. Connect Oura directly; Garmin data comes in through Apple Health. Bluetooth heart rate straps work too.

TRACK YOUR PRACTICE

Streaks, a session heatmap, mood trends and a health dashboard. Grow from Seedling to Master as your minutes add up. Test your CO2 tolerance with the built-in BOLT test.

BUILT FOR EVERY DAY

4 widgets including lock screen, Live Activity and Dynamic Island, Siri Shortcuts, daily intentions, custom breathing sequences and offline sessions.

FREE AND PREMIUM

Free forever: Box Breathing, Deep Slow Breathing, 2-to-1 and Triangle Breathing, plus the breathing timer, streaks and stats. GroundApp Premium ($29.99 a year) unlocks all 35 techniques, the guided sessions and the daily check-in.

YOUR DATA

Export your data anytime and delete your account in Settings. See exactly what we collect at groundapp.live/privacy.

Health disclaimer: GroundApp is a wellness tool that supports your breathwork practice. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Talk to your healthcare provider before starting a new breathing practice, especially if you have cardiovascular, respiratory or other medical conditions.
