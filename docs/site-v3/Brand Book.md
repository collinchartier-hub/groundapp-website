## Content fundamentals

Write like Collin coaching one person: warm, direct, practical. It should feel like a note from a coach who was in the room.

- **Never use an em dash.** Use a period, a comma or a colon.
- **Write "I" for Collin and "you" for the reader.** Use present tense, short sentences and concrete steps: "Lie down and settle first."
- **Give practices exact numbers:** rounds, seconds, minutes, days. "4 rounds Mon to Thu."
- **Write headings as a plain statement or a reframe**, in sentence case: "A what-if is a threat. A when is a challenge." They are set that way too, never in capitals (GROUNDED and the GROUND letters are names, not headlines). No italic phrase and no coloured word in a headline.
- **Write data plainly,** the way you'd say it: "4.5 s in, 6.5 s out", "Weeks 1-4", "$497". No tracked-caps label above a heading.
- **No emoji.** No hype words: level up, optimize, hack, biohack, holistic, unlock, elevate.
- **Spell names this way:** GroundApp (one word), GROUNDED, GROUNDED Live. The App Store name is "Ground".
- **Client material is anonymized.** Never a client's name, dates or identifying details outside their own PDF.
- **Safety:** this is coaching and education, not therapy or medical care. Crisis language points to a licensed professional and 988 (US). Breath practices note who should check with a doctor first.
- **Ads (Meta):** never imply the viewer has a condition ("Can't sleep?", "your anxiety"). Describe the practice instead: "Breathwork for better sleep."

Lines in use:

> Breathe. Regulate. Ground.

> Your nervous system already knows how to settle. Ground gives it the right breath.

> You're not broken. You're dysregulated.

> If you only read one page this week, make it this one.

> Calm under pressure. On command.

## Visual foundations

**Color**
- **Screens are `night`.** That covers web, ads, slides, the app and PDF covers. Print and worksheets are `paper`.
- **Text:** `text` for headings and `text-2` for reading. `muted` is for notes only. On paper it is 4.3:1, so keep it 14px+ and off `bg-2`.
- **`gold` is a fill:** buttons, tick dots, numerals, rules, bars, breath rings. Gold-toned text (data, numerals, links) uses `gold-text`, which is Terracotta on paper. `gold-em` is retired from headlines.
- **Text on gold is `on-gold`,** the brown-black, never white.
- **The two nervous system states have fixed colors.** Calm and rest are `sage` on `sage-tint`; activated and stress are `clay` on `clay-tint`. Always label them in words too.
- **The dark callout on a paper page is `card-dark` with `on-dark` text.** Use one or two per page at most.
- **Breath phases in GroundApp:** `breath-inhale` (Golden Hour), `breath-exhale` (Terracotta), `breath-hold` (Dried Blood). The breath ring gradient runs hold, maroon, exhale, inhale, amber, exhale, hold.
- **No neon, no cool blues or purples, no pure black or white.** The app's blue and purple mood and category colors are off-palette; don't copy them into new work.

**Type** (Collin, 2026-10-15, kit v5)
- **Headlines: Source Serif 4 Regular (400), sentence case,** optical size 60, leading 1.05 to 1.1, no tracking. Heroes, section headings, PDF covers, part bands and page headings. One plain statement: no italic and no coloured word. Cues and pull quotes use it too, at text size: it is the voice for what Collin says.
- **Everything else: Ground Font Type,** Ground's own typeface: Commissioner (by Kostas Bartsokas, SIL Open Font License) with flare 40 built in, the subtle feet at the ends of the strokes. 400 for reading, 500 for card and practice titles, 600 for buttons and bold lead-ins.
- **Counts, timers, prices and dates are Ground Font Type too,** in tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`). The colon centres between figures by itself (04:00). Big breath counts use Light (300).
- **Breath marks:** inhale (U+E010), hold (U+E011) and exhale (U+E012), typed as characters, each before its count. They exist only in Ground Font Type; any other font shows an empty box.
- **The GROUND letters** (GROUNDED hero, GROUND Reset cards) are Source Serif 4 Bold (700), centred in their rows. **The GROUNDED wordmark** keeps Source Serif 4 Light caps.
- **On night,** keep small Ground Font Type at Regular or Medium: thin type glows and blurs on dark screens.
- **No italic for emphasis,** and none in headlines; emphasis comes from scale. Ground Font Type has an italic (its slant axis) for the rare title in running text.
- **Retired:** Source Code Pro and light capitals for headlines (2026-10-15; files in `fonts/_retired/`), and before them Fraunces, Hanken Grotesk, JetBrains Mono.
- **Sizes:** screen styles (`hero`, `h2`, `lead`, `body`, `count`, `timer`, `data`, `breath`) and PDF styles (`print-*`) are in tokens.json.
- **Files:** `fonts/SourceSerif4-latin.woff2` (or Google Fonts), `fonts/GroundFontType.woff2` for the web and `fonts/GroundFontType[FLAR,slnt,wght].ttf` to install on a computer (double-click it). Both SIL Open Font License (`fonts/OFL-*.txt`). Ground Font Type is not on Google Fonts: load it from the kit. Source and build: `~/dev/ground-type`.
- **Inside the iOS app,** keep Apple's New York and SF Pro (`app-*`) until an app update moves the app onto Ground Font Type.

**Layout**
- **Web:** 1100px `container`, body paragraphs held to `measure` (620px), `section` padding 112px, generous space.
- **PDF:** US Letter, `page-margin` 0.72in, a running head and the folio in Ground Font Type, and one idea per page. A cover or part band in `night` opens each part.
- **A section opens with its heading.** No kicker, eyebrow or short rule above it.
- **Number things (1, 2, 3) only when they are a real sequence.** Never 01 / 02 / 03 as decoration.

**Shape and depth**
- Buttons use `radius-button` (14px). Chips and tags use `radius-pill`. PDF and web cards use `radius-card` (12px). Feature cards on the web use `radius-feature` (4px). App glass cards use `radius-app-card` (16px).
- Depth comes from warm gold glow (`glow`) and hairline borders, never grey drop shadows.

**Motion**
- **The breath rings are the signature.** Five concentric gold rings grow and shrink on a box-breathing cycle (`breath-cycle`, 16s).
- **Use slow ease** (cubic-bezier .37, 0, .63, 1) and gentle 30px rises.
- **Breath-paced motion is Ground's alone** (GroundApp, GROUNDED, GROUNDED Live). Other brands don't borrow it.
- **Every moving thing has a reason.** No fade-up copied onto every section.
- **Respect reduced motion:** the rings stop.

**Imagery**
- Use the lung, breath rings and waves as the visual language. Photos are real photos of Collin (see Photography): the retouched studio portrait, race and run-home shots.
- Never use the original unretouched studio portrait.
- In PDFs, bake fades and overlays into the image file instead of using CSS masks.
